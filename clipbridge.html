How it works

Simple by design

ClipBridge runs a tiny local server on your Mac. Your Android app connects over Wi-Fi and keeps clipboards in sync — nothing leaves your network.

01

Mac server starts

Install the ClipBridge daemon on your Mac. It runs in the background and advertises itself on your local network via mDNS.

02

Android connects

Open the ClipBridge app on your phone. It auto-discovers your Mac on the same Wi-Fi network — no IP address to type.

03

Clipboard stays in sync

Copy anything on either device and it appears instantly on the other. Text, URLs, and formatted content — all synced over an encrypted WebSocket.

End-to-end encrypted, local-only

Every clipboard payload is encrypted with AES-256 before it leaves your device. Traffic never touches the internet — it stays entirely on your local Wi-Fi. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

Features

What you get

Everything clipboard sync should be — fast, private, and out of your way.

Sub-second sync

WebSocket connection means clipboard updates arrive in under 200 ms. Copy and switch — it's already there.

Clipboard history

Browse the last 20 entries synced from either device. Tap any item to re-send it to the other clipboard.

Auto-discovery

No IP address needed. The Android app finds your Mac automatically via Bonjour/mDNS on the same network.

Sync on wake

Phone screen on? Clipboard catches up automatically. No need to open the app — it syncs the moment your phone wakes.

Rich text and URLs

Syncs plain text, rich text with formatting, phone numbers, emails, and URLs. Links stay tappable on Android after arriving from macOS. Longer content is chunked and reassembled without truncation.

Setup guide

Ready in 3 minutes

Install once on each device. After that, it just works.

1

Install via Homebrew

Open Terminal and run:
brew install clipbridge

2

Start the daemon

Run clipbridge start — it launches in the background and auto-starts on login. You'll see a tiny icon in your menu bar.

3

Allow clipboard access

macOS will prompt you to allow ClipBridge to access your clipboard. Click Allow. This is required for the sync to work.

4

Note your pairing code

Click the menu bar icon and note your 6-digit pairing code. You'll enter this in the Android app to authorize the connection.

1

Download ClipBridge for Android

Install from the Play Store or download the APK directly.

Get on Google Play
2

Connect to the same Wi-Fi

Make sure your phone and Mac are on the same local Wi-Fi network. ClipBridge uses local discovery — it won't work over mobile data or a different network.

3

Find your Mac

Open the app — your Mac should appear automatically in the device list. Tap it to connect.

4

Enter the pairing code

Type the 6-digit code shown in the Mac menu bar icon. The connection is authorized and clipboard sync begins immediately.
